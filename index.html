Holder Tier Reward

GOATz XRPL Holder Tier Reward is a holder-based NFT reward system. There are no tokens and no forced locking. Rewards are given to holders who genuinely believe in the GOATz ecosystem and choose to keep holding their NFTs. Instead of locking NFTs, the system uses a Proof of Hold mechanism to reward loyalty and long-term support.

How It Works

  • Your GOATz NFTs remain in your wallet
  • Benefits are calculated based on the total number of GOATz you hold
  • A snapshot is taken before the next mint phase
